Before reporting visual work (pages, dialogs, mockups) as done, open and inspect every screenshot of your own output. Look closely for defects such as wrong backgrounds, misaligned or overlapping elements, nested cards, clipped content, empty steps or sections and inconsistent patterns. Critique the design itself: elements that look out of place, irrelevant or duplicate content, and components that do not reuse the shared ones. Also run an adversarial subagent review of it. Fix what you find and iterate until you are fully satisfied; the user should not be the one who spots it.
