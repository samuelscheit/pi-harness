Treat slow or blocking tooling (landing, test runs, CI, builds) as a top-priority defect, not as a state to report or wait out. When agents repeatedly wait, or a step takes hours, intervene without being asked: measure where the time goes, consult an oracle if needed, and fix the cause. Iteration speed is one of the most important drivers of progress, as long as correctness is kept.
